Tools for your creative journey

Creator resources

Quick guides and practical reminders to help you publish responsibly and protect your creative work.

Before you post checklist

Do I own this content? If not, have I checked the source, licence and permission requirements?

Attribution guide

Record the creator's name, work title, source and licence conditions when attribution is required.

Permission record

Keep written permission, licence receipts and relevant correspondence in a safe place.

Using online images

Do not assume an image is free because it appears in a search result or on social media.

Music and video tips

Check whether the audio or footage is cleared for your platform, purpose and commercial use.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use anything found online? No. Check rights and licence terms first.

Is credit always enough? No. Permission may still be needed.

Useful places to learn more

Start with official and reputable sources. Check that guidance is current and relevant to Botswana before relying on it.

  • World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO) — international intellectual property education and resources.
  • Creative Commons — learn how open licences work and what their conditions mean.
  • Companies and Intellectual Property Authority (CIPA), Botswana — Botswana intellectual property information.
This website is an educational resource, not a substitute for legal advice. Always read the full terms of a licence and check the law that applies to your situation.
