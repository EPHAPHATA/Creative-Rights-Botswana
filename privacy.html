Privacy Policy

This page explains the website's intended approach to visitor information and privacy.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Information collected

This educational website is designed to provide information without requiring visitors to create accounts. The contact form in this demonstration website does not transmit or store submissions. If a live form, analytics or other services are added later, the information collected should be clearly explained here.

How information may be used

If contact functionality is connected in the future, information submitted by visitors may be used to respond to questions, provide requested information and improve the website. It should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate basis.

Cookies and analytics

This version does not include analytics or advertising trackers. Hosting providers or services added later may use technical logs or cookies; this policy should be updated to describe them accurately.

Sharing and security

Personal information should not be sold. If information is collected through a future service, reasonable safeguards should be used and access should be limited to people who need it.

External links

This website may link to external resources. Those websites have their own privacy practices, and visitors should review their policies.

Your choices and contact

If the website begins collecting personal information, provide a clear contact method for privacy questions and requests, and update this policy to explain applicable rights and procedures.

Website owner note: Before publishing, replace or expand this sample policy to match the actual hosting provider, contact details, cookies, form service and data practices of the live website. Seek appropriate legal guidance for compliance in Botswana.
