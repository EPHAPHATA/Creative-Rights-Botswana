About the project

About Creative Rights Botswana

An educational website designed to raise awareness of copyright and plagiarism among content creators in Botswana.

Why this website matters

Digital platforms make it easier to create, share and reuse photographs, music, videos, writing, graphics and other media. However, creators may not always understand the rights attached to these works or the difference between acceptable use, copyright infringement and plagiarism.

Our purpose

The purpose of this project is to raise awareness by providing clear, accessible information that helps content creators make responsible decisions when using media content for personal or commercial purposes.

Who the website is for

  • Social media content creators and influencers.
  • Photographers, videographers and graphic designers.
  • Musicians, writers, bloggers and digital publishers.
  • Students, entrepreneurs and other people who use online media.
Our message: Create original content, acknowledge other people's contributions and seek permission when required.
